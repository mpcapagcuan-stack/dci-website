Academic Programs

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT)

The BSIT program focuses on practical skills in software development, system administration, networking, and multimedia applications. Students gain hands-on experience through lab work and industry projects.

Career Opportunities

  • Systems Administrator
  • Web / Mobile Developer
  • IT Support Specialist
  • Multimedia Specialist

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS)

The BSCS program emphasizes theoretical and practical aspects of computing, including algorithms, software engineering, artificial intelligence, and research methodologies.

Career Opportunities

  • Software Engineer
  • Data Scientist / Analyst
  • Researcher / AI Engineer

Program Comparison

Program Main Focus Sample Career Duration
BS Information Technology Application development, systems, multimedia Web Developer, Systems Admin 4 years
BS Computer Science Theory, algorithms, AI, research Software Engineer, Data Scientist 4 years

Facilities

Computer Laboratory

High-performance workstations, virtualization servers, and development tools for software and systems projects.

Main equipment: PC workstations, servers, networking switches.

Networking Laboratory

Hands-on networking lab for routing, switching, and security experiments.

Main equipment: Routers, managed switches, packet analyzers.

Multimedia / Research Studio

Studio space for multimedia production, research, and collaborative projects.

Main equipment: Cameras, audio equipment, editing workstations.
